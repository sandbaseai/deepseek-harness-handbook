Six release gates.

  • 01Images retain their existing intake and rendering behavior.
  • 02Failed batches publish no partial durable message.
  • 03References contain no browser or Host filesystem path.
  • 04Cross-Session and cross-tenant reads are denied.
  • 05Extraction provenance and truncation survive replay.
  • 06Reconnect restores file cards without re-uploading bytes.
