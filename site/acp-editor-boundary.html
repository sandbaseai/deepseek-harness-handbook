What stays off the wire.

Progressraw deltas + reasoning

No live tokens, thinking, tool cards, plans, titles, or usage.

Historyload + resume + fork

Host contextMCP + extra roots

Use a disposable source-demo probe.

Zed can launch a custom ACP process, but that is not proof DSH is a registry-supported editor integration. Keep stdout protocol-pure and credentials outside editor settings.

A complete bridge needs three contracts.

Stream retry-safe deltas and stable tool lifecycles; resume one exact persisted Session without duplicate replay; and advertise only capabilities whose workspace, MCP, permission, trust, and billing boundaries DSH actually enforces.
