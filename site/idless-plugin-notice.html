Preserve the correlation set.

  • 01Stop every writer and copy the complete Session directory.
  • 02Hash the original; work only on an isolated copy.
  • 03Capture the id-less event and its earlier agent/inbox/spliced insertion.
  • 04Find the producer from source.plugin, exact text, package version, and installed bytes.
  • 05Use one consistent identity across correlated events; never patch only the final load error.
  • 06Preserve the one-line header frame, checksums, sequence, surface, and cold-load behavior.
