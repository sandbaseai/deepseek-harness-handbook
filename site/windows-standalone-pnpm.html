Continue only when the probe ends in .js, .cjs, or .mjs. rc.8 also requires Node ^22.19.0 or >=24.0.0.

Do not enable a shell.

Native pnpm.exe should be spawned directly; JavaScript entrypoints should be wrapped with Node. A .cmd shim needs resolution to a shell-free target.
