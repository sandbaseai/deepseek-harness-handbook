Six protocol gates.

  • 01Focus never mounts, unmounts, or reorders panes.
  • 02Every pane reads the bundle for its explicit Session id.
  • 03Opening at capacity never evicts implicitly.
  • 04Closing presentation never mutates durable Session state.
  • 05Async interactions retain their originating Session identity.
  • 06Unknown protocols and unverified plugins fail closed to one pane.
