Safe topology

Choose the configuration owner explicitly.

01SSH loopback

Best for one operator. The browser sees 127.0.0.1 and the shipped scope remains coherent.

02Remote observer

Keep configuration on the Host and expose only reviewed remote capabilities.

03Admin service

Add authenticated authorization, write-only secrets, audit, revocation, and CSRF controls.

04Do not rewrite

Trusted Host, TLS, and forged loopback headers do not create an authenticated principal.
