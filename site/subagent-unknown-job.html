Recovery

Recover the plan, not the stale handle.

01Freeze

Preserve exact acknowledgements, notices, Session identity, and partial files.

02List

Use job_list from the same parent as the authoritative live registry.

03Reconcile

Classify every planned artifact as complete, partial, missing, or conflicting.

04Replace

Rerun only missing units, then gate assembly on a complete manifest.

Do not poll an invented identity.

If the exact Job ID is absent, retries cannot recreate a process-local control record. Preserve completed work and recover only the missing unit.
