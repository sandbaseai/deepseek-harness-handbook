Six proof gates.

  • 01Every process sharing the Session and storage roots is quiescent.
  • 02A tested whole-root snapshot predates mutation.
  • 03The immutable header matches ID, cwd, and artifact path.
  • 04Trash publication cannot overwrite an earlier generation.
  • 05Remaining Sessions list and replay after restart.
  • 06Restore proves the exact digest before retention expires.
