## Measurement rules

  timestamp and exact count.
- Do not attribute a star change to one channel without an observable referral,
  discussion, or other supporting signal.
- Record failed or unwelcome distribution attempts as well as successful ones.
- Keep private analytics, tokens, and personal data out of this file.
