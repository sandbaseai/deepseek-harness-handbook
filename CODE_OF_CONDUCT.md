# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the DeepSeek Harness Handbook community to be a useful, safe place for developers to exchange source-backed documentation and reproducible evidence. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users are expected to participate without harassment or discrimination.

This project is maintained independently by SandBase and is not an official DeepSeek AI community. The same conduct expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient with people of different experience levels and languages.
- Critique claims, evidence, and changes rather than people or motives.
- State verification boundaries honestly; correct mistakes without hiding their history.
- Protect private information in logs, Sessions, screenshots, paths, prompts, and credentials.
- Disclose relevant affiliation when recommending a product, service, plugin, or resource.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, source quality, and promotional content.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing or soliciting another person's private information without permission.
- Fabricated runtime evidence, undisclosed paid promotion, automated engagement, or deceptive claims of official endorsement.
- Repeated advertising, link drops, or off-topic promotion that does not resolve the documented Agent task.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of credentials, private Session data, or production workspaces.
- Disrupting discussions after a maintainer has set a clear project boundary.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary documentation disagreements, open a [handbook bug report](https://github.com/sandbaseai/deepseek-harness-handbook/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) with the smallest relevant evidence.

For sensitive conduct or security matters, do not open a public issue. Use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include only the information needed to investigate, and redact credentials, private prompts, personal paths, and unrelated Session content.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, and other participation that violates this policy. Depending on severity and recurrence, they may warn a participant, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban an account from project-managed spaces. Reports will be handled as privately and consistently as practical; retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in all project-managed spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project. Conduct outside those boundaries is governed by the relevant platform or community.
